Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to your device Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > Spire > Cancel Subscription. Your access will continue until the end of the current billing period.

How do I delete my account?

Contact us at support@typeshiclan.com with your registered email address and we will delete your account and all associated data.

How does the family budget feature work?

Create a family group and invite members via a shared link. Everyone can track expenses, set spending limits, and work toward shared savings goals together.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use Row Level Security to ensure your data is only accessible to you and your authorized family members.

How does receipt scanning work?

Take a photo of your receipt. The app uses on-device text recognition (OCR) to read the receipt, then AI categorizes the expense automatically. Your receipt images are not stored on our servers.
