Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Spire ("we", "our", or "the app") is a household budget management application. This policy explains what data we collect, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It is also the information notice for users in Türkiye under Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and for users in the EU/UK under the GDPR.

1. Who is responsible

Spire is published by an independent developer based in Türkiye. The contact for all privacy matters is supportspireapp@gmail.com. We are the data controller for the data described here.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address and a password (if you sign up with email), or the name and email address Apple or Google share with us when you use Sign in with Apple or Google. With Apple you may hide your email; we then receive an Apple relay address.
  • Display name: the name you choose (or that Apple/Google provides). Family members see you by this name.
  • User-entered data: income, expenses, balances, budgets, savings goals, bills and subscriptions that you enter into the app.
  • Purchase information: subscription status and purchase history processed by Apple or Google and by RevenueCat. We never see your payment card.
  • Family data: which family you belong to, your role, and the family invite code (see section 6).
  • Usage data: feature usage and screen views (Firebase Analytics and Amplitude), linked to a pseudonymous account ID.
  • Diagnostic data: crash reports and performance data (Sentry), which may include your IP address and device and operating-system details.
  • Abuse-prevention data: when a family code is entered incorrectly, we briefly store your account ID and IP address (see section 6).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the app and sync your data across your devices
  • To authenticate your account and manage your subscription and family access
  • To categorize expenses with AI when you use receipt scanning
  • To keep the service safe, for example to stop guessing of family codes
  • To find and fix bugs and to improve the app
  • To answer support requests

Legal bases: performing our contract with you (providing the app), our legitimate interest in security and in improving the app, and your consent where the law requires it (for example push notifications, which you can switch off in your device settings).

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase servers with encryption in transit and at rest. Row Level Security ensures your personal data can only be read by you, plus the limited family data described below. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes by our authentication provider.

5. AI Receipt Scanning

Receipt images are read on your device. Only the extracted text is sent to an AI service (OpenAI) to categorize your expenses. Your original receipt images are not stored on our servers.

6. Family Budget Feature

  • Members of a family see each other’s display name and the email address of the account, and the family data you choose to share (shared expenses, goals and limits). Your balance is shared only if you switch that on.
  • A family has one invite code. Whoever has the code can join the family (up to 5 members). The owner can reset the code at any time and the old code stops working immediately.
  • We keep a record of which account joined which family and when, for 90 days, to limit how often seats change.
  • When a code is entered incorrectly, we store the account ID and IP address of that attempt for 24 hours to prevent code guessing. These records are then deleted automatically.
  • When you leave a family or are removed, your personal transactions stay with you. Premium that came from the family ends.
  • We do not send invitation emails. Invitations are shared by the family owner by any channel they choose.

7. Third-Party Services

These providers process data on our behalf, only to run the app:

  • Supabase: database, authentication and server functions
  • Apple and Google: Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, and in-app purchases
  • RevenueCat: subscription management
  • Firebase (Google): push notifications and analytics
  • Amplitude: product analytics
  • Sentry: error tracking and crash reporting
  • OpenAI: AI analysis of receipt text

Some of these providers are located outside Türkiye, including in the EU and the United States, so your data may be processed there. We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising or tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and all your data yourself in the app: Profile → Delete Account. Deletion removes your account, your data and your family records immediately; copies in provider backups disappear on the providers’ normal backup rotation. You can also ask us to delete data at supportspireapp@gmail.com. Abuse-prevention records are deleted after 24 hours and family join records after 90 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Spire is not directed at children under 16 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your Rights

Under KVKK (Article 11) and the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Learn whether we process your data and request information about it
  • Access, correct or delete your data (deletion is available in the app)
  • Receive a copy of your data in a usable format, on request
  • Object to processing and withdraw consent at any time
  • Complain to your data protection authority (in Türkiye: the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK; in the EU: your national supervisory authority)

Write to supportspireapp@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will tell you about significant changes in the app. The date at the top shows the latest version.
