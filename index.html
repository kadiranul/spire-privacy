Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 29, 2026

Spire ("we", "our", or "the app") is a household budget management application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of data:

  • Account Information: Email address used for authentication and account creation.
  • User-Entered Data: Income, expenses, budget settings, savings goals, and bill schedules that you voluntarily enter into the app.
  • Purchase Information: Subscription status and purchase history processed through Apple App Store or Google Play Store.
  • Usage Data: App interaction data such as feature usage and screen views to improve the app experience.
  • Diagnostic Data: Crash logs and performance metrics to identify and fix technical issues.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's functionality
  • To authenticate your account and sync your data across devices
  • To process and manage your subscription
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs
  • To provide customer support

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase servers with encryption in transit and at rest. We implement industry-standard security measures including Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure your data is only accessible to you.

4. AI Receipt Scanning

When you use the receipt scanning feature, the text extracted from receipt images is processed by an AI service to categorize your expenses. Only the extracted text is sent for processing — your original receipt images are not stored on our servers.

5. Family Budget Feature

If you use the family budget feature, your shared budget data (expenses, goals, and limits) is visible to family members you invite. Each family member must create their own account. You can leave a family group at any time.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Backend infrastructure and data storage
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management
  • Firebase: Push notifications and analytics
  • Sentry: Error tracking and crash reporting
  • OpenAI: AI-powered receipt text analysis

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Data is only shared with the third-party services listed above for the sole purpose of providing app functionality.

8. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at support@spire-app.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Spire is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the app or via email.
